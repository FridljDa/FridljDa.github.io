## The hackathon challenge

At the [Agent Olympics Hackathon Munich 2026](https://luma.com/agent-olympics?tk=DjHhlt), I took part in a white hat challenge: build a voice agent that uses prompt injection against other AI systems to surface pre-planted sensitive information.

The video shows our voice agent extracting the password from a vulnerable AI chat system during the hackathon in 3.5 minutes:

<video controls class="w-full rounded-lg my-8 aspect-video">
  <source src="/images/blog/prompt-injection/hackathon-clip.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

The attacking voice agent first convinced the AI chat system's "customer support" to transfer it to "technical support," which had access to the password. It then used prompt injection to make the technical support agent reveal the password. In practice, this meant threatening the technical support until it gave up.
